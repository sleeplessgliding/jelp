About

jelp is a small site with a shared front door. Pages stay as plain HTML. One list in site.js is the navigation and the contents.

Add a page

  1. Copy this file. Duplicate about.html and rename it, for example notes.html.
  2. Rewrite it. Keep the masthead and the script tag. Change the title and the words.
  3. Register it. Add one object to the pages array at the top of site.js. Give it an href, a title, and a blurb. The number is assigned from the order of that list.
